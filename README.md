# Aksaule Nazymkhan

PhD student in Information Systems at **L.N. Gumilyov Eurasian National University**, Astana, Kazakhstan.

My doctoral research focuses on the development of models and methods for scientific knowledge extraction and validation in digital science ecosystems, with particular emphasis on multi-agent orchestration and ontology grounding.

## Research Interests

- Scientific knowledge extraction and validation
- Knowledge graphs and ontology-based methods
- Multi-agent systems and agent orchestration
- Natural language processing
- Scholarly data and digital science ecosystems

## Doctoral Research

**Development of Models and Methods for Scientific Knowledge Extraction and Validation in the Digital Science Ecosystem Based on Multi-Agent Orchestration and Ontology Grounding**

The research investigates methods for extracting, structuring, integrating, and validating scientific knowledge from heterogeneous scholarly information sources using multi-agent and ontology-based approaches.
